Convert once. Store anywhere. Stream everywhere.

iVault is a desktop app that turns a video file on your computer into a playable streaming link (HLS) on storage you already own, then hands you the URL.

Get helpHow your data is handled
1 · Drop

Add your episodes

Drag in one file or a batch. Set the series and episode number.

2 · Encode

HLS in four sizes

FFmpeg on your machine makes 360p to 1080p renditions. Nothing is uploaded to us.

3 · Upload

To your storage

Segments go straight to the provider you connected, with resumable, checkpointed jobs.

4 · Copy URL

One stable link

Paste the master.m3u8 link into your site or player.

Your storage, your keys

iVault has no account and no server of its own. It talks only to the services you set up, using credentials kept in your operating system's keychain.

  • Cloudflare R2
  • Amazon S3
  • MinIO
  • Telegram
  • Google Drive
  • GitHub
  • OneDrive
  • WebDAV
  • Local folder / NAS
