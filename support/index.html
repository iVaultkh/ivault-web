Common questions

iVault says FFmpeg is missing

iVault encodes video with FFmpeg on your computer. On macOS and Linux, Settings → FFmpeg can download a verified build for you. On Windows, download the “release essentials” zip from gyan.dev, unzip it, and in Settings → FFmpeg choose its bin folder (the one containing ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe).

How do I connect Telegram?

Create a bot with @BotFather and copy its token. Make the bot an administrator of a private channel. Post any message in the channel, forward it to a bot such as @userinfobot or open the channel in Telegram Web: the channel ID is the number starting with -100. Paste both into Storage → Telegram.

Uploads to Telegram are slow

Telegram limits bots to roughly 20 messages a minute and about 20 MB per downloadable file. iVault stays under those limits on purpose, so a long episode takes a while. The job resumes where it stopped if you close the app.

A job failed halfway

Every stage is checkpointed. Press Retry on the job: iVault skips encoding and segments that already uploaded.

Where is my data?

On your computer, and in the storage you connected. See the privacy policy for details.
