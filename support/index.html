Common questions

iVault says FFmpeg is missing

iVault encodes video with FFmpeg on your computer.

  1. macOS and Linux: Settings → FFmpeg can download a verified build for you.
  2. Windows: download the “release essentials” zip from gyan.dev, unzip it, and in Settings → FFmpeg choose its bin folder (the one containing ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe).
How do I connect Telegram?
  1. Create a bot with @BotFather and copy its token.
  2. Make the bot an administrator of a private channel.
  3. Find the channel ID: post any message in the channel and forward it to a bot such as @userinfobot, or open the channel in Telegram Web. The ID is the number starting with -100.
  4. Paste the token and the ID into Storage → Telegram.
Uploads to Telegram are slow

Telegram limits bots to roughly 20 messages a minute and about 20 MB per downloadable file. iVault stays under those limits on purpose, so a long episode takes a while. The job resumes where it stopped if you close the app.

A job failed halfway

Every stage is checkpointed. Press Retry on the job: iVault skips encoding and segments that already uploaded.

Where is my data?

On your computer, and in the storage you connected. See the privacy policy for details.
