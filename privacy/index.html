Privacy policy

Applies to the iVault desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, including the Microsoft Store version. Last updated 26 September 2026.

In short

iVault has no account, no server and no analytics. Your videos, settings and credentials stay on your computer. The app sends data only to the storage and metadata services you connect, and only to do what you asked.

Who we are

iVault is published by iVaultkh (ichamrong) under the MIT License. Questions about this policy go to the contact channel on the support page.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run a service that receives data from the app. iVault contains no telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not create a user account with us.

What stays on your device

  • Your library and jobs: file paths, series and episode details, upload progress and the links iVault produced, in a local database in the app's data folder.
  • Encoded video: temporary HLS files while a job runs.
  • Credentials: storage access keys, bot tokens, OAuth refresh tokens and API keys are stored in your operating system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain or the Linux Secret Service), never in plain files.

Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all of this. Credentials can also be removed from inside the app by disconnecting a provider.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

iVault only makes network requests needed for features you turn on. Each service below receives data under its own privacy policy, using an account that belongs to you.

We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we never receive it.

Your content

You are responsible for the videos you upload and for having the right to store and share them. Links iVault creates are as public as the storage you point them at: a public bucket or public repository makes the video reachable by anyone with the link.

Children

iVault is a tool for publishing video and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Security

Credentials are kept in the OS credential store and are removed from error messages and logs before they are shown. Connections to services use HTTPS where the service offers it.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.

Contact

Open an issue on the support page and mark it “privacy”.
